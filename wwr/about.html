River Rush Rafting

At River Rush Rafting, our mission is simple: turn first-time nerves into lifelong memories. Every trip we guide is built on respect for the river, genuine care for our guests, and a passion for adventure that never gets old.

History

River Rush Rafting was founded over a decade ago by a small crew of river guides who couldn't imagine doing anything else for a living. What started as weekend trips for friends grew into a trusted outfitter known for expert guides, well-maintained gear, and routes suited for everyone from nervous beginners to thrill-seeking veterans.
